Mentorship

Support for students exploring research and geospatial careers.

Who I can help

Undergraduate and early graduate students interested in GIScience, GeoAI, high-performance geocomputing, geospatial standards, or human mobility analytics.

What mentorship can include

Research topic discussion, paper reading groups, project scoping, coding practices for spatial analysis, and guidance on conference or graduate-school preparation.

How to reach out

Send a short email with your background, interests, and what you hope to work on. I am happy to chat when capacity allows.
