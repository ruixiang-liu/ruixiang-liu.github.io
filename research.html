Research

Themes spanning Autonomous GIS, spatial reasoning, geospatial big data, and human mobility— grounded in interoperable standards and high-performance computing.

01 / AUTONOMOUS GIS

Autonomous GIS & Spatial Reasoning

Designing agentic geospatial systems that parse intent, reason over spatial knowledge, and support interoperable geospatial intelligence services.

02 / GISCIENCE

GIScience & Big Data Analytics

Building methods and infrastructures for large-scale geospatial analysis, discovery, and decision support across heterogeneous data ecosystems.

03 / GEO-COMPUTING

High-performance Geo-computing & Data Cubes

Accelerating spatial workflows with parallel and distributed computing, including real-time ingestion and processing in geospatial data cubes for Earth digital twins.

04 / MOBILITY

Human Mobility

Modeling multi-scale mobility patterns and adaptation—such as responses to extreme heat— using large mobile location datasets and spatial analytics.

05 / GEOAI STANDARDS

Knowledge Graphs & GeoAI Standards

Advancing semantic geospatial data discovery and contributing to OGC TrainingDML-AI and related standards for AI/ML training data interoperability.
