Research

My research connects geospatial data discovery, scalable computing, and mobility analysis, with a focus on knowledge graph-driven agents for Autonomous GIS.

01 / AUTONOMOUS GIS

Autonomous GIS & Spatial Reasoning

I study how large language models and multi-agent systems can support geospatial data discovery and retrieval. Current work includes GeoAutoRetriever and a systematic review of spatial reasoning in large language models, both in preparation.

02 / GISCIENCE

GIScience & Big Data Analytics

My work on the Open Geospatial Engine (OGE) examines analysis-ready spatial data infrastructure and geospatial services. Related studies address spatiotemporal data governance for intelligent connected vehicles.

Analysis-readiness through OGE · IJRS, 2026 →

03 / GEO-COMPUTING

High-performance Geo-computing & Data Cubes

I work on real-time ingestion and processing in geospatial data cubes for digital twins of Earth. RTGDC and my work on a cube-enabled cloud geoprocessing engine connect geospatial data management with high-performance computing.

RTGDC · International Journal of Digital Earth, 2024 →

04 / MOBILITY

Human Mobility

I use mobility data to study responses to heat and the connections between cities. Recent collaborations examine heat thresholds and neighborhood inequality, and use mobility traces to identify gaps in intercity services. A first-author study of mobility responses to U.S. heatwaves is under major revision.

Heat and neighborhood inequality · Big Earth Data, 2026 →

Directional latent demand · Computational Urban Science, 2026 →

05 / KNOWLEDGE GRAPHS

Geospatial Knowledge Graphs

Knowledge graphs connect geospatial data sources and support intelligent discovery. This thread runs from identifying links in the Web of data to a knowledge graph-driven multi-agent framework for geospatial data discovery.

Linking geospatial data sources · ISPRS IJGI, 2024 →

06 / GEOAI STANDARDS

GeoAI Standards

I contribute to standards for GeoAI training data and geospatial data preservation. I am an editor and submitter of the OGC TrainingDML-AI JSON and XML encoding standards, and a submitter of its conceptual model. Related research addresses GeoAI training data and interoperable geospatial agentic services.
