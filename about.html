About

Geographer working at the intersection of autonomous GIS, spatial reasoning, and human mobility.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Geography at The Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. Zhenlong Li in the Geoinformation and Big Data Research Laboratory (GIBD).

Before joining Penn State, I earned an M.Sc. in Cartography and Geographic Information System and a B.Eng. in Remote Sensing Science and Technology, both from Wuhan University. My master's research was supervised by Prof. Peng Yue.

My research connects autonomous GIS and spatial reasoning with knowledge graphs, geospatial data cubes, and high-performance computing. I also study human mobility, including responses to extreme heat using mobile location data.

Alongside my research, I contribute to geospatial standards through the Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC) and national standardization efforts in China.
